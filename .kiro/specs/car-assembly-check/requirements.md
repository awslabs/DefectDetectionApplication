# Requirements Document

## Introduction

`car-assembly-check` is a DefectDetectionApplication (DDA) use case, not a platform feature: a camera photographs a three-piece puzzle of a car (front fender, rear fender, roof) placed in a fixture, and the system gives a fast pass/fail verdict per image on whether every piece sits in its single valid position. When the verdict is fail, the output identifies the misplaced piece(s) with an image mask. The model is an RF-DETR-Seg instance-segmentation model with three classes (`front_fender`, `rear_fender`, `roof`) that only segments the pieces; correctness is decided by a post-processing **layout check** that compares each predicted piece mask against that piece's known valid position (a stored **reference layout**). The training set therefore needs no examples of wrong placements to *define* correctness; the wrong-placement photos that do exist (22 device captures of the same fixture, Facts) join the labeling job as further examples of the same three classes — no "misplaced" class — and form the Fail_Set that validates the verdict (owner decision O13, resolved). The 48 photos first prepared for training are **not** all correct assemblies (owner statement, 2026-11-24): a per-image triage of every image (`placement-triage.csv`, Facts) is the ground truth for which images are correct, which pieces are misplaced or missing, and where a hand or other occluder is in the frame; only triage-confirmed correct images feed the reference layout and the false-fail measurements. Photos are converted to 8-bit single-channel grayscale (Pillow mode `L`) before training, and the same grayscale conversion is applied to camera frames on the device before inference so inference input matches training input. **"Monochrome + on-device grayscale" is the production default.** By the owner's decision (O12, resolved), a colour-trained variant is also trained from the same labels and the two models are compared on the same held-out images and the same device (Requirement 11); the result of that comparison confirms or revises the grayscale-on-device decision.

Everything the use case needs from DDA for instance segmentation — the `InstanceSegmentation` labeling modality, the Instance_Manifest and COCO RLE dataset, RF-DETR-Seg training launched from the Portal, the device decoder and result contract for per-instance masks, mask display in the device HMI and the Portal Results tab — is specified by `rfdetr-seg-support` (`.kiro/specs/rfdetr-seg-support/`). ## Glossary

Terms of `rfdetr-seg-support` keep their meaning: Instance_Modality, Instance_Manifest, Canonical_RLE, Instance_Result, Detection_List (list form and map form), Composite_Mask, Output_Mode, Seg_Stage_Type (`rf_detr_instance_segmentation`), Seg_Size, Seg_Switch, Seg_Feature_Floor (`WORKFLOW_RFDETR_SEG_MIN_LOCAL_SERVER_VERSIONS` — its value per-deployment configuration since owner decision O18 (ii), with the committed literal as the default, Requirement 9.3), LocalServer_Image. Terms of the workflow specs keep theirs: Detection_ID, Run_Metadata, Node_Type_Catalog, Executor_Binding, Scene_Analytics_Node, Static_Image_Camera, Workflow_Component, Component_Packager. New terms:

- **Piece**: one of the three physical puzzle parts. **Piece_Class**: its model class, exactly one of `front_fender`, `rear_fender`, `roof`, in this order (class indices 0, 1, 2).
- **Assembly_Image**: one camera frame (or dataset photo) of the fixture with the pieces placed. **Training_Orientation**: the orientation of the `assembly-photos/` images — the car upright, roof at the top, the fixture's suction-cup feet at the bottom; the device captures are rotated 180° from it (Facts).
- **Triage_File**: the owner-confirmed `placement-triage.csv` (Facts; 70 rows, confirmed 2026-11-25; two columns added 06:16 UTC), one row per image with `filename, set, source_path, verdict, misplaced_pieces, missing_pieces, occluder, confidence, notes, piece_counts, unlabeled_stray`; `verdict ∈ {correct, incorrect, incorrect_and_missing, missing}`, `occluder ∈ {none, hand}` where the literal `none` (or an empty cell) means no occluder, piece lists `;`-separated, **`piece_counts`** = `front_fender=N;rear_fender=N;roof=N` (the exact number of instances of each class the labels must hold), **`unlabeled_stray`** = the number of pieces outside the car outline (never labeled). **Known_Good_Set**: the images whose row says `correct`, names no occluder (`occluder` empty or `none`) — a stray piece outside the car outline (`unlabeled_stray > 0`) does not exclude a row, because the Layout_Check ignores a detected piece outside the car outline (owner decision O19, Requirement 5.3), so `c3889f82` and `dc7bf42a` are Known_Good. **Fail_Set**: the images whose row says the placement is wrong or incomplete (`misplaced_pieces` or `missing_pieces` non-empty), with or without an occluder or a stray piece — the 22 wrong-placement captures and the 11 of the 48 the triage marks incorrect. **Occluded_Correct_Set**: the images whose row says `correct` but names an occluder — a hand over a correctly placed car, i.e. something in the frame the check has no class for (no row today: the three `hand` rows are Fail_Set rows); the layout check cannot be expected to fail them, so they carry **no verdict expectation**, are reported separately, and feed neither the Reference_Layout nor either rate (Requirement 1.7). The three sets partition the labeled images; their sizes are stated once in the Facts (37 / 33 / 0) and referred to from everywhere else.
- **Labeling_Job_Of_Record**: the one DDA labeling job whose polygons are labeled to completion and feed every downstream artifact (the labeled manifest, the Derived_Manifest, both training jobs, the Reference_Layout, the calibration) — **`r2` = `labeling-485efbd9`** (`car-assembly-instances-r2`, created 2026-11-25 19:43:59 UTC from the prepared request with the colour `prompt_overrides`; its id read from the demo Portal's jobs table at 19:46 UTC and re-checked against the updated `labeling-job-r2.md` and `GET /v1/labeling/<id>` by tasks 1.1b, never invented), by owner decision **O17 (A)**, resolved 2026-11-25. **`r1`** (`labeling-f85bdfc7`, `car-assembly-instances-r1`) is the other job: created, unlabeled, **stopped (not deleted) at 19:49:01 UTC** after `r2`'s auto-label completed (19:46 UTC). Both jobs enumerate the same 70 objects under `assembly-photos/`. The job of record derives every path that depends on it: the labeled manifest **`labeled/labeling-485efbd9/output.manifest`** (the DDA worker's key `labeled/<job id>/output.manifest`; the persisted `output_manifest_s3_uri` is authoritative), the operator-named Derived_Manifest key **`labeled/car-assembly-instances-r2-monochrome/output.manifest`**, the Pre-Labeled Dataset name, the Colour_Variant's dataset. A further round **`car-assembly-instances-r3`** could be created by the separate r3 run on the fixed Grounded-SAM worker (PR #172) if its pre-labels beat `r2`'s (the parent session's instruction to that run, 2026-11-27 05:27:34 UTC, after the owner's O15 (i) ruling; O15 (ii), which would have required one, was withdrawn); **that run did not create it** (`/home/ubuntu/github/jp5/main/.agents/tasks/car-assembly-check/labeling-job-r3.md`, 2026-11-27 06:19 UTC: no prompt map beat `r2`'s pre-labels; Facts "Decision O15 and the r3 run"). The file is re-read at execution time: should it later record an `r3` (the owner may still create one), its id and whether it is the job of record are taken from that file only and re-checked against `GET /v1/labeling/<id>` — never invented; if it is, it takes over the role and every derived name (`labeled/<its job id>/output.manifest`, `labeled/car-assembly-instances-r3-monochrome/output.manifest`, the Pre-Labeled Dataset, the Colour_Variant's dataset), and `r2` is stopped by that run on the owner's word, never by this spec.
- **Labeled_Set**: all 70 images of the Labeling_Job_Of_Record (`r2`, O17 (A); the same 70 objects `r1` enumerates — decision O14 resolved (a): the 22 joined the 48 under the same prefixes).
- **Reference_Layout**: the single valid position of every Piece, represented as one Expected_Region per Piece_Class.
- **Expected_Region**: a closed polygon in normalized frame coordinates (x, y in [0, 1], origin top-left, 3 to 64 vertices) describing where a Piece's mask lies when the Piece is correctly placed. Same coordinate convention as the `zone` parameter of `detection_counter` (`catalog/nodes.py` `_ZONE_DESCRIPTION_PREFIX`).
- **Layout_Check**: the post-processing node type (`type_id = layout_check`, display name "Layout Check") and its executor binding, which compares the Instance_Results of a run with the Reference_Layout and emits a Layout_Verdict.
- **Layout_Verdict**: `pass` or `fail` for the whole Assembly_Image, plus one Piece_Status per Piece_Class.
- **Piece_Status**: `ok` (exactly one **counted** instance of the class — confidence ≥ `min_confidence` and not outside the car outline — with Position_Score ≥ `min_iou`), `misplaced` (exactly one counted instance, Position_Score < `min_iou` or no usable mask), `missing` (no counted instance), `duplicate` (more than one counted instance). A qualifying instance (its class configured, confidence ≥ `min_confidence`) whose Outline_Overlap is below `min_outline_overlap` lies **outside the car outline** and is **ignored**: not counted, never flagged (owner decision O19, Requirement 5.3). `pass` iff every configured class is `ok` (the three Piece_Classes in this use case; the node itself checks whatever classes its Reference_Layout names).
- **Position_Score**: the intersection-over-union between a predicted instance mask (decoded from its `mask_rle` at source-frame resolution) and the Piece's Expected_Region rasterized at the same resolution.
- **Car_Outline**: the part of the frame the assembled car occupies when every Piece is correctly placed — the **union of the Reference_Layout's Expected_Regions**, rasterized at the frame resolution with the rule of Position_Score. It is the runtime form of the labeling policy's "car outline" (the fixture's recessed outline, which the three pockets form): the Reference_Layout is captured from a Known_Good image, where the three pieces fill the three pockets (Requirement 4.4).
- **Outline_Overlap**: for one detected instance whose mask decodes and covers at least one pixel, the fraction of its mask that lies inside the Car_Outline (|mask ∩ Car_Outline| / |mask|, in [0, 1]); compared with the node parameter `min_outline_overlap` (Requirement 5.3).
- **Misplaced_Mask**: the predicted mask (`mask_rle`) of an instance whose Piece_Status is `misplaced` or `duplicate` (an ignored instance is never one); with the Expected_Region of every Piece whose status is not `ok`, it is what the result views render on `fail`.
- **Grayscale_Node**: the `custom_python_preprocess` node carrying the fixed Grayscale_Handler (Requirement 6) placed immediately before `model_inference`.
- **Grayscale_Model**: the RF-DETR-Seg model trained from the monochrome images (`car-assembly-seg-gray`); the production default. **Colour_Variant**: the second model trained from the colour originals with the same labels (`car-assembly-seg-color`), compared with the Grayscale_Model in Requirement 11 (decision O12, resolved by the owner); the comparison's winner is bound as the production model.
- **Derived_Manifest**: the Instance_Manifest obtained from the labeled manifest by rewriting every `source-ref` prefix from the labeled image prefix to the grayscale prefix (`assembly-photos/` → `assembly-photos-monochrome/`), the key's remainder unchanged, nothing else changed. Under O11 it is the manifest the Grayscale_Model trains on; it covers the whole Labeled_Set (70 lines), and a grayscale copy of every labeled image (the 48 and the 22) exists under the grayscale prefix with the mirrored key (Facts).
- **Batch_Run**: the procedure that runs a deployed workflow once per image over a set of local images through a `folder_source`, one file at a time, driven by `samples/car-assembly-check/batch_run.py` **on the device host**, and exports one record per image — the run's `layout` section, node durations, and the run preview's `instances` (the Detection_List with `mask_rle`, `GET /workflows/executions/{id}/overlay`) — (Requirement 10.3, design §13). **Result_Export**: its output file.
- **Run_Upload**: the device-side upload of a finished run's artifacts to `<thingName>/<workflowId>/<executionId>/` in the inference-results bucket, specified by `results-tab-workflow-runs` FR-D1 (not implemented as of 2026-11-24). Requirement 8 reads what it uploads.
- **Verification_Device**: the Jetson on which Requirement 10 is performed — **`mic-730ai`**, the demo account's Jetson AGX Xavier (`arm64_jp5`, JetPack 5.1.3; owner decision O6 (β), 2026-11-25). **Demo_Account**: AWS account 326334468650 / us-west-2 (profile `dda-usw2`), the account of the Portal, the use case `reinvent`, the bucket and the Verification_Device; Greengrass component versions are per account, so a version number is meaningful only with its account (Facts); the demo `arm64JP5` lineage and the Verification_Device are **shared** with another active actor (Facts, "shared lineage"). **Capture_Camera**: the Basler acA4600-10uc (`Basler-267601652282-23405186`) on thor2 (`zone3-show-thor-dda`) that took the 70 photos; the Verification_Device has no camera (decision O16 (a): none is mounted for this use case). **Pinned_Revision**: a revision of the Verification_Device's thing-targeted deployment submitted through the Portal's `POST /deployments` with every row of the current deployment resubmitted at its current version and the LocalServer row at an **explicit** `component_version` (`<DEMO_JP5_VERSION>` for the move, `<PREVIOUS_VERSION>` for a rollback) — never the UI's "Update to latest", which selects whatever is highest on the shared lineage at the click (Requirement 9.4). **Pre-move read** and **`<PREVIOUS_VERSION>` / `<PREVIOUS_DEPLOYMENT_ID>` / `<PREVIOUS_REVISION>`**: the state of the shared device read immediately before the move's POST and recorded in the notes — the installed `arm64JP5` version (`list-installed-components`), the deployment id and revision to be superseded (`list-deployments --history-filter LATEST_ONLY`) and its full component set (`get-deployment`); the only source of the rollback target and of the body's other rows. 6. **[O3, O13]** THE `min_iou` of the Layout_Check node SHALL be calibrated in two steps, both through `layout_reference.py --calibrate` with the Triage_File (`--triage`) deciding which images are Known_Good and which are Fail_Set, and the node's default 0.70 SHALL never be deployed uncalibrated. **(a) From the labels, before the first deployment:** `--calibrate <manifest> --regions <json> --triage <csv>` computes, for every labeled image and class, the Position_Score of the labeled mask against the Reference_Layout and prints, **over the Known_Good_Set**, per class the minimum, 5th percentile, median and maximum (the `test`-split rows separately), the synthetic margin rows — each fender's score against the *other* fender's Expected_Region (the swapped-fenders case) and the roof's score against a **vertically flipped** copy of its region (an upside-down roof, flat side up; Only after that re-run may a pass/fail case of Requirement 10.2 be recorded as a result. The `--results` form SHALL re-derive every verdict with the `min_outline_overlap` that the record's `layout` section echoes, select the best instance per label among **counted** instances only (an ignored instance is never scored against a region), report the Outline_Overlap margin — per set, the minimum over counted instances and the maximum over ignored ones — and list every instance whose Outline_Overlap lies in the band [0.05, 0.50) as "near the car outline" for the owner (the threshold is a policy value; no labeled instance lies near the outline in the data); it SHALL refuse (exit 1, nothing computed) an export any record of which echoes `min_outline_overlap = 0` — the outline rule off — while the Known_Good_Set holds rows with `unlabeled_stray > 0`, because their pass expectation rests on the rule. WHEN the camera or fixture moves, THE user SHALL recapture it (criteria 3–6) — the documentation (Requirement 12.5) SHALL say so, and the Layout_Check's node status SHALL make a systematic shift visible (every piece `misplaced` with similar scores) so the condition is recognizable; a frame in the wrong orientation (a camera frame not rotated into the Training_Orientation, Requirement 6.5) shows the same signature and is the first thing to check. ### Requirement 5: The Layout_Check node on the device

**User Story:** As a workflow author, I want a post-processing node that turns the three predicted piece masks into a pass/fail verdict with a per-piece status, so that device outputs and result views get a fast, explainable answer without a second model.

Depends on rfdetr-seg-support: Req 9.4–9.5 (Instance_Results with `mask_rle`/`mask_area` at source resolution; tasks 9.1–9.3, done), Req 10.1–10.2 (the Detection_List entries carry `mask_rle`; the marshal writes the capture record and the `<id>.detections.json` sidecar; tasks 10.1–10.2, done), Req 10.5(c)/(d) (`detections.read_detections` returns the raw entries with `mask_rle`; `build_detection_list` strips it; task 10.5, done), Req 10.3 (`detections_truncated` / per-entry `mask_truncated`; task 10.1, done). Implementable and unit-testable now; on a device only inside a LocalServer built with this spec's changes (Requirement 9.4: the demo-account `arm64JP5` Portal Build Fleet build of `main` after this spec's merge) — the Seg device contract it consumes is hardware-verified on JP6 (that spec's 22.1–22.2), exercised on `mic-730ai` (its JP5 record) and the Seg floor is deployed (20.2 done), so nothing of that spec blocks it; the first run of this spec's device code on hardware is on JP5 (Python 3.11 in the `arm64JP5` image; the shared module is stdlib-only). Pattern extended: `detection_counter`/`object_association` (`rtsp-rtmp-stream-cameras` Requirements 13–14: shared pure logic in `workflow_core/analytics/`, executor hookup in `scene_analytics.py`, sandbox mirror in `test-sandbox/harness/bindings.py`, catalog descriptor, validator rules V13/W3, inline-check mirror).

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. **[O2]** THE Node_Type_Catalog SHALL gain a post-processing node type `layout_check` ("Layout Check"): one `in` and one `out` port of type InferenceMeta, `hardware_dependent = False`, an executor binding on every architecture (`_same_on_all_archs(executor_binding="layout_check")`, no GStreamer element), with parameters `expected_regions` (string, required, the Reference_Layout JSON of Requirement 4.1), `min_iou` (float, optional, default 0.70, constraints 0.01 ≤ value ≤ 1), `min_confidence` (float, optional, default 0.5, 0 ≤ value ≤ 1), **`min_outline_overlap` (float, optional, default 0.10, constraints 0 ≤ value ≤ 0.5 — the fraction of an instance's mask that must lie inside the Car_Outline for the instance to count; 0 counts every instance; owner decision O19, criterion 3)**; descriptions and examples in the catalog's style. The descriptor SHALL be identical in the Portal layer and the vendored device mirror (the mirror test passes), and the CSI golden of `nodes.py` SHALL be rebaselined in the same commit (rfdetr-seg-support Corrections §1).
2. **[O2, O3]** THE shared pure function (stdlib-only, no I/O, never raises — the `scene.py` convention) SHALL take the raw Detection_List entries (list form, with `mask_rle`), the frame size, the parsed Reference_Layout, `min_iou`, `min_confidence` and `min_outline_overlap`, and SHALL return `{pass, pieces, flagged, ignored, unexpected_classes, expected_regions, frame, min_iou, min_confidence, min_outline_overlap, outcome, problems}` where `pieces` maps each key of the Reference_Layout to `{status, iou, detections: [{id, class_label, confidence, bounding_box, iou, outline_overlap}]}` (`detections` always a list: empty for `missing`, one entry for `ok`/`misplaced`, every qualifying instance for `duplicate`; `iou` at the piece level is the best instance's score or null), `flagged` lists the Detection_IDs whose masks are Misplaced_Masks, `ignored` lists the qualifying instances excluded as lying outside the car outline (`[{id, class_label, confidence, bounding_box, outline_overlap}]`, criterion 3), `unexpected_classes` lists detected class labels absent from the Reference_Layout, `expected_regions` echoes the Reference_Layout used (so result views need no access to the workflow definition), `frame = {width, height}`. Position_Score SHALL be computed at source-frame resolution by rasterizing the Expected_Region into the same column-major run representation as Canonical_RLE and intersecting runs, never by materializing a `width × height` pixel array in pure Python; Outline_Overlap (criterion 3) SHALL be computed the same way, on the union of the rasterized Expected_Regions; the design states the algorithm and its cost.
3. **[O3, O19]** Piece_Status rules: qualifying instances of a class are the entries with that `class_label` and `confidence ≥ min_confidence`. **The outline rule (owner decision O19):** a qualifying instance whose mask decodes to `frame.width × frame.height` and covers at least one pixel has an Outline_Overlap — the fraction of its mask inside the Car_Outline, the union of the Reference_Layout's Expected_Regions at frame resolution; if that fraction is below `min_outline_overlap`, the instance lies outside the car outline and SHALL be **ignored**: listed under `ignored` with its Outline_Overlap, not counted, never flagged, and `outcome` unchanged. Every other qualifying instance is **counted** — an instance whose Outline_Overlap cannot be measured (no mask, undecodable, zero area) is never ignored. Over the counted instances: zero → `missing` (iou null), more than one → `duplicate` (all flagged), exactly one → `ok` if iou ≥ `min_iou` else `misplaced` (flagged). **Consequences:** a piece lying outside the outline does not change the verdict; a piece set down outside the outline instead of in its pocket leaves its class `missing` when no counted instance of that class is inside, so the image still fails; a piece in a wrong pocket, or partly out of its pocket with at least `min_outline_overlap` of its mask inside, is counted exactly as before; `min_outline_overlap = 0` ignores nothing (every qualifying instance counts, the behaviour before O19); a `min_outline_overlap` that is not a number in [0, 0.5] (impossible after validation, criterion 6) is replaced by the default 0.10 with `outcome = warning` and a problem naming the value. `pass` iff every configured class (every key of the Reference_Layout — the three Piece_Classes in this use case) is `ok`. An entry whose `mask_rle` is absent (`mask_truncated` — not expected at this payload size, Facts) or does not decode to `frame.width × frame.height` SHALL be treated as having no mask: it counts as qualifying for `duplicate`/`missing` purposes, its iou is null, its status is `misplaced`, **`outcome` becomes `warning`** and a problem names the Detection_ID (so the text reaches the node status, which carries problems only on `warning`/`error`); a run with no Detection_List at all (no detection record) SHALL yield `pass = false`, every piece `missing`, `outcome = warning` ("no detections to check") — mirroring `count_detections`' warning rule; a qualifying instance whose class label is not a key of the Reference_Layout SHALL be listed in `unexpected_classes` with `outcome = warning`, wherever it lies (the outline rule applies to configured classes only) (it means the reference omits a class the model knows — a configuration error that must not pass silently); an unknown frame size SHALL yield `outcome = warning` ("frame size unknown") with every piece `missing`; an unparsable `expected_regions` (impossible after validation, Requirement 5.6, but checked) SHALL yield `outcome = error` with the problem, which gates the executor bindings wired **directly** downstream of the Layout_Check node without failing the run (the `scene.py` rule; `output_bindings._gated_out` checks a binding's own `upstreamNodeIds` only).
